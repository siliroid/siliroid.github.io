Eight axes, twenty-four facets. The product is where the two wings cross — the read you can't get by taking a personality test and an attachment quiz separately.

It isn't a quiz

The free door is twelve questions and it's honest about what that is: a litmus test for roughly where you'd land. The assessment is a different object. About a hundred and twenty-five items, twenty-five real minutes, facet structure underneath, and validity signals that read a contradiction as a self in motion rather than a failed lie test.

Every pole is described fairly. Not every pole is flattered.

Both ends of every axis are written to be recognisable — the oh god, that's me kind — and each one carries its own real failure mode. Warmth curdles into performed care. Plainness curdles into contempt. There is no good side to land on, which is the only way a reading like this is worth anything.

And it will talk you out of it

If the reading says you need constancy — that being able to reach something whenever you want it is how you experience care — you get told, in plain words, before you're asked to pay: this will frustrate you, and here's what fits you better.

That gate lowers conversions on purpose, and it's written into the specification so it can't quietly be removed in year two. An instrument that can't tell you no isn't reading you.

What you actually get

  • A folio, twelve to eighteen pages, assembled for your exact pairing — not a template with your type name dropped in.
  • Three parts: the people you bond with, the presence you hold, and where those two meet and argue.
  • Your fingerprint — eight bars and twenty-four facet marks, including the ones sitting balanced, which are a real place to be and get written as their own third thing.
  • Yours to keep. Download it, share the code, retake it on a calmer day.

It gets truer the longer you're here

Every instrument like this is self-report, and self-report is what you believe about yourself on a Tuesday.

Ours is too — and then it keeps going. Once you're paired, behaviour re-scores both wings, and the gap between who you said you were and how you actually held the bond gets shown to you, kindly, if you ask for it.

No other instrument can offer that, because no other instrument lives with you.
